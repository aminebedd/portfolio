ExcellenceSchool

نظام إدارة المدرسة الخاصة — School Management System

A complete school management system built for Algerian private schools, covering all education levels from 1ère Année Primaire to 3ème BAC. The system manages 3 roles: Admin, Teacher, and Student — each with their own dashboard, features, and permissions.

Next.js 14 TypeScript Tailwind CSS shadcn/ui Django 5 DRF SimpleJWT PostgreSQL
GitHub Live Demo

Problem & Solution

Problem

Private schools in Algeria manage everything manually — attendance, schedules, subscriptions — leading to errors and lost data.

Solution

A centralized digital platform that automates daily school operations with role-based access for admin, teachers, and students.

Key Features

Admin Control Center

Full control over teachers, students, classes, timetable, and subscription plans (monthly or session-based), with attendance override and notifications.

Teacher Tools

Teachers mark attendance per class and can cancel sessions, automatically triggering notifications to affected students.

Student Portal

Students view their personal weekly schedule, receive notifications, and track their attendance history.

Plan Expiry Detection

Automatic detection of expiring or expired subscription plans, with notifications sent to admins and students.

Weekly Timetable

Full CRUD timetable management for admins, covering every class, level, and speciality.

Algerian School Week

Built around the Algerian school week (Sunday to Thursday) to match real institution schedules.

Configurable Levels

All education levels and specialities — from 1ère Année Primaire to 3ème BAC — are configurable from settings.

Responsive & Dark Mode

A fully responsive interface across devices, with built-in dark mode support.
